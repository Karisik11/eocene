# eOcene
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Slovenian secondary-school students tracking school results and study time on phones and desktops.
## Product Purpose
A free personal school workspace combining grades, average planning, calendar, studying, optional ranking and peer tutoring.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve all existing behavior, authentication, account/privacy controls, APIs, data, subject management, calendar statuses, Pomodoro audio and focus mode, achievements, wallpaper selection and administration. Preserve user data and all unmodified behaviors. Explicitly requested functional refinements accompany the 25.0 UI redesign. Preserve public hosting and domain. Never invent real student data or marketing claims.
## Brand Commitments
eOcene, Slovenian UI, blue and white with optional dark mode, compact readable surfaces, custom subject symbols, gentle motion. User delegates the visual remake based on earlier requests. Avoid oversized cards, empty space, emojis and semicolons in user-facing copy.
## Product Principles
Keep core tasks easy to find. Keep personal results legible. Preserve user control and working functions. Adapt to every screen.

## Current visual commitment
The user selected a calm iOS-inspired redesign on 24 October 2026: blue accents, white and charcoal themes, larger desktop type, readable grouped cards, left-aligned grade and subject copy, and unchanged calendar content. Follow Apple HIG as design guidance, not as a claim that this web app is a native Apple application.
